Florr.io

Florr.io is a massively multiplayer survival and exploration game where you control a flower. You navigate a massive map, defeat various insect-like mobs, and collect "petals" to upgrade your abilities, health, and attacks.

Core Mechanics

Gameplay: You start with a basic set of petals and earn XP by fighting mobs. As you progress, you unlock higher-level zones.

Petals: These act as your attacks, shields, and passive buffs. They range in rarity from Common to Unique, and you can craft duplicates together to create stronger, rarer petals.

Mobs & Drops: Defeating enemies yields new petals. The more dangerous the mob, the better the loot. Drops are shared in PvE, meaning multiple players who participate in the fight get rewarded.

Multiplayer: You can play solo or team up with other players to take down difficult bosses and high-rarity mobs.

Background

It was developed by Matheus Valadares, who is also known for creating other popular browser games like Agar.io and Diep.io.

You can jump in and play for free directly in your web browser on the Florr.io website.
