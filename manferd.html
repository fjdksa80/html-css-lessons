Manfred

Manfred is the most famous pro player in Florr.io. He is known throughout the community as a legendary player who has achieved some of the highest power levels in the game.

Who is Manfred?

Manfred is a highly active, elite player known for his extreme dedication to grinding and his in-game wealth.

The "P2W Lord": Community members often call him the "Pay-to-Win (P2W) Lord" or "Manfraud" because he has spent thousands of dollars on the game. However, players note that he also spends an incredible amount of time actually playing and grinding for his loot.

Famous Playstyle: He is known to play for hours daily, often seen AFK farming through elite Super mobs.

Community Jokes: He is sometimes jokingly called "Mangreed" because he actively "greeds" (targets and hoards) rare Super mob spawns.

Notable Achievements

Unique Petals: Manfred has successfully forged and owned several incredibly rare Unique petals, including items from the Sewer maps.

Unique Battles: He famously won a massive "Unique Clover" crafting war against another top player, -Sam8375, which temporarily pushed the game's crafting requirements to new limits.

First Coin: When the "Coin" petal was stealth-released by the game developer, Manfred was the first player ever to be given one.

Many players joke that Manfred's financial support is the main reason the solo developer, M28, continues to update and develop Florr.io!
